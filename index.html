A simple approach to a new challenge, a team project, or the next idea worth exploring.

  1. Understand first.

    Ask the right questions. Get clear on the goal, the people involved, and what actually matters.

  2. Make a plan.

    Turn a broad ambition into manageable steps. Set priorities and give the work a clear direction.
